## What changed

Provider I/O for detection now goes through `web/src/lib/ai/`. `detect-llm.ts` still builds prompts, parses classifications, paces, retries, switches models on daily quota, grounds quotes, and chooses primary vs miss-only.

- `InferenceUsage` / `parseOpenAiCompatibleUsage` read Groq `prompt_tokens`, `completion_tokens`, `prompt_tokens_details.cached_tokens`, and `completion_tokens_details.reasoning_tokens`. Absent, invalid, or incomplete prompt/completion counts stay `null` with `status: "unknown"`. Cached and reasoning tokens are not added into the other counts.
- `noopAttemptSink` is the production default. `HybridDetectOptions.attemptSink` receives one `InferenceAttempt` per fetch, emitted from `finally` after success, HTTP failure, network failure, timeout, malformed JSON, and classification shape failure.
- `attemptId` is a UUID created in `postGroqChatCompletion` immediately before `fetch`. `logicalOperationId` is one UUID per `classifyConversationWithGroq` call. `attemptOrdinal` starts at 1 and increments across retries, fallback switches, and the extra fetch when a concurrent call already switched the session.
- `detectRouteVersion()` is the card's route version: `detect:<primary|miss-only>:<primaryModel>|<fallbackModel|off>`. Classification context and `Signal.detectionContext` gain `stage: "detect"` and `routeVersion`. `model` and `promptVersion` are unchanged. `detectionRunId` is left unset for CB-06.
